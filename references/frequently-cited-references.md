# References frequently cited in Duan-first-author FAS/HOFAS papers

## Corpus and counting method

The count uses 23 title-deduplicated Duan-first-author PDFs/manuscripts found in the user's Zotero library on 2026-09-08. Duplicate attachments of the same paper and duplicate copies of the 2026 monograph were excluded.

Each frequency is the number of distinct corpus papers whose References section contains the work. DOI matching was used when possible; normalized author-title matching was used for books and chapters. Because PDF line breaks and OCR can split a DOI/title, the reported numbers are conservative lower bounds rather than bibliometric absolutes.

## 3. Which reference to use for which claim `[D]`

| Claim in a new manuscript | Preferred citation type |
|---|---|
| Definition/basic procedure of HOFAS | Duan Part I or the 2026 monograph |
| Conversion of generalized strict-feedback systems | Duan Part II |
| Robust or adaptive HOFAS | Duan Parts III–V |
| Complete parameterization/eigenstructure assignment | Duan Part VII plus the exact earlier parametric-design source used by the theorem |
| Standard nonlinear stability fact | Khalil or Isidori, then the exact theorem/edition |
| Feedback-linearization comparison | Isidori |
| Nonholonomic stabilization obstruction | Brockett's original chapter |
| $H_2/H_\infty$ or robust linear design | Zhou–Doyle–Glover or the exact source cited by Duan |
| Output regulation | Saberi–Stoorvogel–Sannuti or the exact source cited by Duan |

## Citation discipline

1. Cite the original mathematical result when Duan himself cites an earlier theorem; do not cite only the HOFAS paper for a standard lemma.
2. Cite a Duan first-author source for Duan's terminology and notation.
3. Do not infer endorsement or importance solely from raw frequency; some papers share long background bibliographies.
4. Before submission, verify edition, year, volume, pages, title capitalization, and DOI against the rendered PDF or publisher metadata.
5. Frequency in this file is for navigation, not a claim that the list is a complete citation analysis of all Duan publications.
